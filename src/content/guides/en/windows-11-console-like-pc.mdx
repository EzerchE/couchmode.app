A Windows 11 gaming PC feels more console-like when the controller, display, and launcher follow one predictable start flow. Choose a controller-friendly experience such as Xbox full-screen where it is available, Steam Big Picture, or Playnite Fullscreen. Keep the desktop work around that experience small and repeatable.

Windows remains Windows. The improvement is that a TV session can start without first finding a window, changing the input, dismissing a notification, or launching the same library by mouse.

## Choose the experience that fits your library

Steam Big Picture is a practical choice when Steam is the center of the library. Playnite Fullscreen is useful when games and launchers live in several places. Xbox full-screen can be an option where it is supported by your Windows version, device, and Microsoft rollout. Availability is not identical on every PC, so treat it as an option to check rather than a guaranteed mode.

Open the chosen experience manually first. Confirm that it is readable from the couch, controller navigation works, and the television is on the correct input. A reliable manual path gives you something concrete to compare when automation is introduced.

## Make the controller the beginning of the session

CouchMode can use a compatible controller as the start signal for the gaming experience you choose. The Free plan covers Xbox full-screen where supported, Steam Big Picture, and Playnite. This lets a controller connection begin the part of Windows you intend to use for games without presenting the whole desktop as the first interface.

Use a controller that Windows exposes through the supported compatibility path. Xbox controllers are the clearest starting point. Do not assume that every controller behaves identically: native PlayStation controller connections have a current session-trigger limitation, even though those devices may work in individual games through their own support paths.

## Treat the TV as part of the workflow

A living-room PC has more moving parts than a desk monitor. The TV may be asleep, the receiver may select a different audio device, and Windows may remember a display arrangement from work. Begin with a simple setup: one display path, one compatible controller, and one launcher. Change only one condition at a time when testing.

CouchMode Pro covers optional session automation for available audio, HDR, display, power, notification, resource, and selected app-cleanup choices. It does not increase frame rate or promise better game performance. Use it when those repeated setup steps are part of your routine.

## End the session without losing the desktop

When a CouchMode session ends, the app restores the supported settings it changed. It does not replace the Windows shell, control every application window, or turn a PC into a permanently locked-down console. That boundary is valuable: you can leave the TV setup, return to work, and still have a normal Windows desktop.

If you mainly use Playnite, start with the controller-to-Playnite guide and the focus troubleshooting guide. If Steam is the center of the setup, the Steam Big Picture guide covers the special case where Steam or Big Picture is already open before CouchMode starts.

The official download page has the current Windows release. For real-world controller, TV, receiver, and dock combinations, the CouchMode Reddit community is the best place to compare notes without pretending that every hardware combination has the same behavior.
